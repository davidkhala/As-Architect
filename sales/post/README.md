# Upsell
Upselling is offering more advanced features or higher-tier versions of the existing solutions they already use to solve additional challenges. 
- You must be curious and explore opportunities where your client might benefit from premium services, enhanced features, or additional licenses and/or capacity to meet their growing business needs.

# Cross-sell
Cross-selling is offering clients new products or services that complement the solutions they’ve already acquired. 
- Cross-selling includes suggesting related products that could solve related business challenges to enhance the overall value of their current investment.
