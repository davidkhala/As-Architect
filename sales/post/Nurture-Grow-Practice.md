# Nurture
Nurture is about establishing trust and credibility, personalizing interactions, and maintaining regular engagement with clients to build a solid foundation.
- Strong client relationships act as a barrier to entry for competitors, making it harder for them to poach clients.

## principles
Client Centered
- Focus deeply on discovery to understand root cause of client challenges.
  - e.g. coming prepared to every discovery opportunity having done your homework on the client, their industry, their strategy.
- Share your expertise
  - share in a way that is aligned to them and relevant to their challenges
- Explore and validate the expected outcomes: POC alike

IBM Team Initiated
- Engage with authentic curiosity
- Be transparent, even when the news isn’t good
- Hold a focus on long-term partnership
  - Rather than just short-term sales

Value Based
- Partner with the account and client team to focus on the outcomes and business impact.
- Deliver to maximize value throughout the entire client lifecycle
  - not just when there is something new to sell them (offering oriented)
- Assess value realized.
  - Insist on ensuring the client achieves their expected outcomes and business value **after implementation**.


## Be transparent and honest
> Trust is built in drops and lost in buckets
- Deliver on promises, admit mistakes when they occur, and proactively address delays, issues or challenges.
- Communicate them openly and timely with the client.
- Offer solutions or alternatives to mitigate any negative impact whenever possible.


## Consistent communication
Stay in regular contact with your clients after the sale is complete.
- Provide updates(e.g. product developments), offer support, and address any concerns that may arise
- Schedule regular meetings (e.g. quarterly business reviews)
## Encourage training and onboarding support
Offer ongoing training, workshops or support for the client’s team to ensure they are maximizing the benefits and value from their solution(s)

## Proactively problem-solve
Offering solutions before problems arise (e.g., a software update that may impact their workflow) reinforces trust

## Celebrate milestones and successes
> 86% of buyers will pay more for a better customer experience, particularly in B2B
Celebrate together.
- Acknowledge significant achievements in the client's business (e.g. successful product launch or a milestone in their digital transformation) that are tied to your solutions

## Seek and leverage client feedback
Conduct surveys or formal feedback sessions or solicit informal feedback.
## Share expertise and resources
as trusted advisors by demonstrating expertise in their industry
- continually staying informed about industry trends and challenges
  - Educational content: whitepapers, analyst reports training sessions that educate clients on the broader applications and value of software technology.
  - Host: Invite clients to webinars, panel discussions or roundtable events with industry experts to discuss relevant industry or technological topics that your client will care about.
- offering valuable insights and advice, solutions and best practices.
  - Implementation strategies and best practices: Share proven strategies and best practices for implementing and maximizing the value of software solutions through guides or video tutorials.

## Offer exclusive early access to what’s new
Provide your long-term clients with early access to new products, features, solutions or updates.
