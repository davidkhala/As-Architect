# Customer Success Manager

Customer Success Managers are the frontline technical experts working with our clients post sales. 
- They help ensure a client’s business goals are met by our software and services.
- CSMs deeply understand their client’s business and engage with them regularly both face-to-face and virtually.
- A CSM co-defines the roadmap, conducts workshops, presents demos, facilitates EBRs, and builds customer advocates, and drives deployment at all levels of the account.
